    What is this?

    In 2024 the world earned about $112 trillion. We cut that into 195 equal shares of about $575 billion and drew one new country around each share. The lines follow real district borders (with a few straight cuts), not today's countries.

    Limits

    • Equal within limits: each country earns between 0.94 and 1.04 of a share.
    • The number of people is an estimate from gridded data.
    • This page draws a simplified map. Near a line it can be a few km off. The full data is in the zip below.
    • The line across Manhattan is an estimate from payroll data, not GDP. It runs along 45th Street; other readings put it anywhere from 38th to 45th Street.
    • Names are working names, mostly the biggest city inside. They are not proposals.
    • Lines follow the district files used; they take no position on any border dispute.
